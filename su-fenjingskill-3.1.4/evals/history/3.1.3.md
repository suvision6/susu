# 3.1.3 Evaluation History｜camera-grammar-01

## Upgrade objective

- Reframed the Skill around one job: converting locked script material into a director-readable audiovisual shot table.
- Split the four-file delivery into one human frontend (XLSX) and three Agent backends (JSON, Markdown and validation) while retaining one core fact model.
- Kept the two XLSX worksheets: “导演分镜” for per-shot reading and “导演设计” for the ten-field whole-work summary.
- Restored the useful older discipline of protecting complete action, performance, sound and spatial processes before adding a cut. A new cut must provide a concrete viewing gain over continuing the shot.

## 3.1.3 regression focus

- `shot_flow[]` owns intra-shot time order without introducing a second free-text truth.
- `boundary_to_next` owns the single Gate 2 cut proof through `relation`, concrete `trigger` and concrete `editorial_gain`; the terminal unit has no boundary.
- XLSX column five is a deterministic two-layer projection: one camera header plus one chronological natural-language paragraph.
- Backend `execution_text` remains available to Agents and is not copied into XLSX.
- Dialogue remains verbatim, punctuation-preserving and ordered in both the backend facts and the XLSX projection.
- Ordinary warnings no longer cause `READY_WITH_ASSUMPTIONS`; only real open assumptions do.
- Tests explicitly reject generic word-count, shot-count and shot-duration ceilings.
- Each Gate 2 unit owns a concrete `viewing_design`; the formal shot carries the matching `viewpoint`, framing and visible/offscreen facts.
- Scene `camera_grammar` explains what stays stable, what can change and which source event triggers a change. It does not impose a category quota.
- Listener holds, single shots, over-shoulders, inserts, subjective views and space views are directing choices, not a rotation checklist.
- Identical framing/angle/movement/owner choices require a specific confirmed `uniformity_intent`; copied reasons across different viewing jobs fail as `CAMERA_DECISION_TEMPLATE_COLLAPSE`.
- A schema-valid 3.1.2 migration carrier uses explicit `unresolved` camera states. Semantic validation blocks it with `CAMERA_DESIGN_UNRESOLVED` until the decisions are rebuilt; no viewing choice is inferred from legacy defaults.

## Superseded internal candidate

The earlier `frontend-backend-split-01` candidate proved the frontend/backend projection but failed director-quality review: its EP02 fixture inherited hard-coded two/group-shot framing, all-level angles and fixed/lateral movement. That fixture is evidence of the defect, not evidence that core shot design passed. It is archived and is not an installable candidate.

The `camera-grammar-01` candidate must prove both layers independently:

- technical integrity: source/dialogue preservation, schema, Gate, Alignment, four-file parity and deterministic XLSX projection;
- directing integrity: picture ownership, visible/offscreen allocation, reading scale, framing, angle and camera response arise from the current scene event and method.
